# Privacy Policy: Photographer's Pocket Knife

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

Photographer's Pocket Knife is a macOS app for culling, editing and delivering photos. It is made by David Arsénio Martins ([ividi.dev](https://ividi.dev)).

## The short version

The app does not collect, store or share any personal data with the developer or with third parties. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

## What stays on your Mac

- **Your photos, catalog, edits, ratings and keywords** stay on your Mac, in the folders you choose and in the app's own data folder.
- **Upload destination passwords** are stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac.
- **Settings** (language, theme, shortcuts) are stored locally.

## When the app connects to the internet

The app only connects to the internet in two cases:

1. **Delivering photos.** When you upload photos, the app sends them to the FTP, FTPS, SFTP, WebDAV or S3 server that you set up. The developer never receives your photos or your server details.
2. **Optional AI models.** If you choose to download them in Settings, the app downloads the model files for object removal and smart selection from Hugging Face. It sends nothing about you or your photos; all image processing runs on your Mac.

The version distributed outside the Mac App Store also checks GitHub for app updates. That check sends no personal data.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
